Design motivation

Designed for differences between gyms.

Equipment, layouts and naming conventions can vary substantially between gyms. A fixed exercise list would not describe every machine or match the way each person records training.

“Exercise names, progress measures and training history can therefore be adjusted by each person.”

Activity-specific progress

A relevant measure for each activity.

Progress is not limited to a one-repetition maximum. Depending on the activity, the useful measure may be working weight, assistance, distance or duration.

Technology and privacy

Keeping the public demonstration separate from private records.

The working application requires a login. This public page uses fictional data and local interactions to explain the project, and it does not read or write any private training records.
