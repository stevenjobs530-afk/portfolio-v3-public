Personal Training Website V2

A private training system built around the reality that equipment, exercise names and progress measures vary from gym to gym.

Instead of forcing everyone into one fixed catalogue, the product lets each person create a training library in their own terms, then record strength, cardio and recovery as private history.

Design motivation

Built around the user's gym.

Different gyms can have very different equipment, layouts and naming conventions. A universal exercise list cannot describe every machine or the way each person understands their training environment.

“The product gives users ownership of the vocabulary, metrics and history that describe their training.”

User-defined progress

Track the measure that actually matters for each activity.

Progress is not always a one-rep max. It might be working weight, assistance, distance or duration. Each view can use its own metric and unit.
