Overview

This University of Bristol applied research project examines early-career transition and workplace wellbeing. A public questionnaire connects to a dedicated Supabase backend that validates each submission, isolates formal research data and supports controlled export.

The challenge

A public form had to adapt to different participant situations without giving browsers direct access to the response table. Consent, versioning, routing, selection limits and score ranges all required server-side validation.

Method

From raw questions to reliable evidence.

01

Route

Direct students and recent graduates to B1, professionals to B2 and other visitors to an open route.

02

Validate

Check origin, mode, consent, UUIDs, allowed options, route consistency, score ranges and request size.

03

Protect

Deny response-table access to browser clients; allow the public page to submit only through the server function.

04

Export

Separate test and formal modes; add flattened export and integrity SQL for analysis control.

Decision log

The choices I made—and why they matter.

  • Keep the questionnaire public while blocking browser access to response data.
  • Validate business rules on the server rather than trusting hidden or disabled controls.
  • Version the questionnaire and data structure together for later analytical traceability.
  • Show interface and architecture evidence only—never participant responses.

Outcomes

What the evidence produced.

  • Deployed a seven-stage questionnaire with situational routing.
  • Established a validated Edge Function boundary and RLS-protected storage model.
  • Added export-ready SQL and pre-export integrity checks for reproducible research handling.
